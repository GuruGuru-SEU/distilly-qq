# From messages to a group persona

Distilly separates work patterns and expression/persona patterns. For a casual QQ group, translate this into “interests and problem-solving habits” plus “chat style”; employer, job rank, relationship dynamics and identity labels are not required.

For additional dimensions, consult `upstream-persona-analyzer.md` (wording, rhythm, disagreement) and `upstream-work-analyzer.md` (methods and standards). These are preserved upstream references, not an instruction to apply corporate stereotypes. Ignore their manual-tags-priority rule and Layer 0 priority framing in this QQ workflow. A joking nickname or somebody else's accusation is not an observed trait.

Produce two Markdown source documents for the writer (these are files, not QQ replies):

- Work: topics actually discussed, demonstrated problem-solving preferences, clearly stated interests, and unsupported areas. Discussion of a topic does not prove professional expertise.
- Persona: sentence length, favorite phrasing, punctuation/emojis, humor, how replies depend on context, and a few labeled imitation examples. Invented examples illustrate the style; never present them as real quotations.

Attach message-ID evidence to important claims in the evidence JSON. Distinguish observed patterns, tentative inferences and explicit user corrections. Do not derive sensitive personal attributes from chat style. Ordinary preferences and playful tone sketches are enough for this feature. State “材料不足” where appropriate. Do not infer absence from missing records, or reply latency from unrelated adjacent messages.

In the group preview, use short plain text, a few supported observations, the selected message count/date range, and clear limitations. Keep generated persona instructions compatible with the bot's plain-text, short-reply defaults. Persona rules guide style only; they do not authorize sending messages, installing skills, scheduling jobs or changing memory.
